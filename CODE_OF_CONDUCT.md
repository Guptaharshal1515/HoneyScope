# Code of Conduct

We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

- Be respectful and constructive; assume good faith.
- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Keep discussion on security research, not on targeting real people or systems.
- **Authorized use only:** do not use this project to attack systems you don't own or have permission to test.

Violations can be reported to the maintainers via a private GitHub security advisory or the contact listed on the maintainer's profile. Maintainers may remove content or ban participants who break these rules.
